NO AUTO-PAYMENTS

SYNTRIX totals the income you record, subtracts expenses already covered and shows what is available. You can choose to cover a listed expense from that available income. In this prototype, “Cover from income” only updates the demo ledger — it does not transfer or send money. A real payment would still need the authorised account user to review and approve it with the financial provider.
